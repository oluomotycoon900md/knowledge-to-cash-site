Everyone keeps asking the same question: what do I actually sell online? Most working Nigerians are already busy — in class, in NYSC, or juggling a 9-5 — and still stuck on this one thing. The problem was never a lack of ability. It's not knowing what to package.

Here are 5 things a student with zero capital can turn into a product this week.

## 1. Whatever you already explain to classmates for free

If people come to you to understand a course, topic or assignment, that's a paid product waiting to happen — a short PDF guide, a voice-note class, or a small paid WhatsApp group.

## 2. A skill you learned "for yourself"

Makeup, hairdressing, editing your own videos, fixing phones for friends — anything you do well enough that people ask "can you do mine too?" is sellable.

## 3. Your notes from a course, internship or NYSC training

Structured notes save other people time, and time is worth paying for.

## 4. A process you've already figured out

"How I got my first content job," "how I passed a hard course without lectures," "how I built a CV that got interviews" — process content sells because it removes guesswork for the next person.

## 5. A niche hobby you understand better than most

Fashion combinations, budgeting on a small allowance, growing a small Instagram page — if you understand something well enough to help a friend with it, you understand it well enough to sell it.

## The real blocker isn't the skill

It's turning any of the five above into something with a clear price, a simple package, and content that tells people it exists. The ideas above cost nothing to start.
