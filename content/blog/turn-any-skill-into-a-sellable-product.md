Most people don't fail to make money online because they lack a skill. They fail because the skill was never turned into something with a name, a price and a way to buy it. Here's the process, step by step.

## Step 1: Name the transformation, not the skill

Instead of "I know makeup," say what result you give someone: "I help first-time brides look camera-ready in 45 minutes." A transformation is sellable. A skill on its own is not.

## Step 2: Pick one format you can deliver this week

A skill can become an ebook, a checklist, a short video course, a done-for-you service, or a coaching call. Pick the one you can actually deliver with what you already have — a phone is enough.

## Step 3: Set one price, not a menu

Beginners overwhelm buyers with options. One product, one price, one clear promise converts far better than five vague ones.

## Step 4: Write three posts before you write anything else

A problem post ("why most people get this wrong"), a proof post ("what changed when I did this"), and an offer post ("here's how to get it") is enough content to start selling. You can expand later.
